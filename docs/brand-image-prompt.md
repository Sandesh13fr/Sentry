# Brand guidelines and toolkit: image-generation prompt

Paste into Midjourney v7, GPT-image, Ideogram 3, Imagen 4 or Flux 1.1 Pro. Landscape 16:9, at least 2560×1440. Ideogram and GPT-image handle the text labels best.

---

A single brand-guidelines and toolkit board for "SENTRY", a strategy-driven AI arena with reproducible match verification. It is a flat, print-ready design-system sheet, laid out like a Swiss engineering spec on an off-white page (#FAFAF8) with a faint 22px dotted grid. No photography, no 3D, no gradients, no glow, no drop shadows. Crisp vector shapes only.

Layout: a 12-column grid. Thin hairline dividers (#E8E5DF) split it into labelled modules. Section labels are small, sentence case, in a monospace face.

Top-left module, "Logo". A hexagonal shield mark filled with ember orange #F2541B. A thinner inner hexagon outline sits inside it in white at 55% opacity. A horizontal rounded white "eye slit" bar crosses the centre, with a small orange pupil dot. Next to it, the wordmark "Sentry" in a geometric grotesk like Geist Bold with tight tracking, ink #1C1B19. Show three lockups underneath: the mark on light, the mark on dark (#121110 background, mark #FF6B33), and a 16px favicon. Draw a clear-space rule as dashed guides.

Top-right module, "Colour". Big flat swatch tiles with hex labels in monospace: Ember #F2541B (Fighter A, primary action), Steel #2D62E3 (Fighter B), Ink #1C1B19, Paper #FAFAF8, Surface #FFFFFF, Muted #6F6C66, Line #E8E5DF. Below them, a row of small semantic chips: green #12844A "Verified", red #C2352A "Mismatch", amber #A86200 "Testnet unavailable". Each chip sits on its own pale tint. Add a smaller dark-theme strip: #121110, #FF6B33, #7099FF, #F2F0EB.

Middle-left module, "Type". The headline "Describe two fighters. Watch a match you can replay exactly." in Geist Bold, large, tight leading. Under it, body text in Geist Regular, then a monospace sample in Geist Mono: "seed arena-001 · sentry-sim-1.0.0 · 9f2c41ab…e07d13". Show a type-scale ladder: 40 / 20 / 15 / 13.

Centre module, the hero focal point, "Fighters". Two friendly flat robot fighters face each other on a perspective arena floor. The floor is thin radiating lines, a dashed elliptical ring, an orange centre dot and a small outlined pylon. The left fighter is tinted ember and holds a slim blade. The right fighter is tinted steel and holds a heater shield with a cross. Both have rounded-rectangle helmets with a coloured visor slit, an antenna with a ball tip, a dark ink chassis and legs, and simple boots. Beneath them, three small variant silhouettes are labelled "Striker (aggression)", "Runner (speed)" and "Tank (defence)". Each has a different helmet or visor shape.

Middle-right module, "Effects". Small flat icons in a row: a star-burst hit spark, a jagged amber crit burst with ink outline, two curved grey whiff swoosh lines, a dashed guard ring ellipse and a "KO" tilted-figure pose.

Bottom-left module, "Icons". A 6×3 grid of 24px line icons with 2px stroke and round caps, ink colour: play, pause, replay, dice, check-circle, x-circle, alert-triangle, link, hash, four-point AI spark, cpu chip, search, copy, repeat, crossed swords, trophy, lab flask and empty hexagon.

Bottom-centre module, "Components". Real UI pieces drawn flat. A primary ember button "Run match" with a play icon. Secondary outline buttons "Verify", "Anchor" and "Replay match". A textarea labelled "Fighter A strategy" with a small orange swatch and a "0 / 300" counter. A segmented speed control "1× 2× 4×" with 2× selected in ink. HP bars, one ember and one steel. A 10-pip stat meter "Aggression 8". A monospace fingerprint box holding a 64-character hex hash. Status chips "Engine sentry-sim-1.0.0", "Parser OpenRouter" and "Testnet Monad", each with a green dot.

Bottom-right module, "Voice". Three short do / don't pairs in small text. "Run match" not "Click to start!". "Verified. Re-run matches the saved fingerprint." not "Success!!". "Testnet unavailable. The match is still saved." not "Error 500".

Footer strip in small monospace: "Sentry · brand toolkit v1.0 · academic prototype · no wagering, tokens or real money".

Style: calm, precise, credible, like a developer-tool design system (Linear or Vercel spec sheets) but warmer. Corners are rounded 6, 10 and 14px. There is exactly one orange accent; everything else is neutral. Generous whitespace, perfect alignment and legible labels.

Avoid: purple-blue gradients, neon, glassmorphism, cyberpunk, emoji, crypto coins or tokens, casino or betting imagery, cream-plus-terracotta, black-plus-acid-green, photoreal robots, clutter, and misspelled text.

---

Negative prompt (for generators that take one): gradient, glow, neon, 3d render, photo, bokeh, emoji, coins, casino, dollar, purple, lens flare, watermark, illegible text, extra fingers.
